## Lessons from the stills review
- The stream's banner shifts with every zoom, so themed bars that cover it must ride the camera.
- A HUD or checklist can spoil the card too. Encrypt the name until the reveal.
- Remove duplicate text: when a big kinetic word is on screen, the caption under it goes.
- Rain-of-money emoji looked cheap and was cut. Keep graphics tied to the story.
