# Hooks & retention: what the 50 Shorts do

Back to [[START HERE]] · Data: [[Shorts analysis - 50 clips]] · How to apply: [[Editing playbook]]

Counts are out of the 50 analysed clips (≈ how many clearly showed it on screen).

## 1. The hook is text on screen, at frame 0 (~17/50)
- A **persistent title** at the top that stays for the whole Short. It frames *why* to keep watching:
  - **"Wait for…" / promise of a payoff**: "WAIT FOR PICK #1…" (Ref A), "Wait for it…" (#12).
  - **"Others vs last one"**: "OTHERS WEAPON DETECTION TRAINING", "OTHER FISHING SKILL", "OTHERS IN HYDRAULIC PRESS" (#1 1.4B views, #11, #22, #33). Viewer waits for the odd one out.
  - **Claim to be proven/disproven**: "ISHOWSPEED CAN'T LIFT HEAVY 💀", "SPEED HAS NO STAMINA" (#20, #55).
  - **Ranking**: "Ranking Funniest Ice Cream Moments", with a 1–5 list that fills in (#19, #57). Viewer waits for #1.
  - **Situation teaser**: "…then things got WORSE 😭" (#43), "KIDNAPPING PEOPLE FROM DIFFERENT STATES" (#23).
- Short (3–6 words), uppercase, 1–2 colored keywords, often ends with "…" or 💀/😭.
- **Clipping/podcast channels** use a quoted question as the first caption: "WHOSE ARM IS THIS?", "WHAT WOULD BE YOUR GOLF FOURSOME?". **Lead with the question**, not with small talk.

## 2. The title changes at the twist (~8/50)
At the payoff the top text swaps, usually to **red**: "THIS ONE", "BUT NOKIA…", "ARE YOU SURE?", "LAST ONE". This rewards the viewer for waiting and marks the climax.

## 3. Something new every ~1–2 seconds
- Jump cuts / punch-in zooms between speakers (podcast + streamer clips).
- A new caption chunk every 0.5–1.5 s (2–4 words).
- Stickers/emoji popping in on keywords.
- **Dead air is never left alone.** Silence is cut, or turned into a beat: crickets, "*THINKING*", slow zoom, ⏳.

## 4. A visible climax
- Color **flash/tint** of the whole frame: **green = win/punchline**, **red = fail** (#5, #6, #7, #52, Ref A).
- 💀 / troll face / 😈 sticker slams in (~17/50 use emoji stickers; 💀 is the most common by far).
- Meme counters: **"AURA: -40670 💀"**, "AURA +608", "MISSION PASSED! RESPECT +" (#6, #17, #32, #58).
- B&W freeze or comic filter on the reaction (#4, #5, #20).
- Big label of the punchline noun (Ref A `*JESUS + TIGER*`).

## 5. Reaction is part of the payoff
After the punchline they cut to **who reacts**: the listener's face, the laughing table (#29, Ref A, #31).

## 6. Endings that loop or ask
- Whip/motion-blur transition at the end so it **loops** seamlessly (#23, #34, #35).
- Some end on a CTA ("LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE" sign #51, ❤️ comment #33). Rare; the loop is more common.
- Kai/Speed clips **replay** the funniest moment 2–3× (#53, #54).

## 7. Raw clips still win if frame 0 is absurd (~8/50)
Flood at the door (#8, 95M), gym crowd (#13). No text needed when the image itself is the hook. Not our case: podcast footage needs the text hook.

## Rules distilled for podcast/streamer clips
1. Frame 0: persistent hook title + the question as the first big caption.
2. Never more than ~1.5 s without a visual change.
3. Cut or dress up silences.
4. One loud climax (flash + sticker + SFX + title swap), then the reaction.
5. End fast (≤1 s after the last line) or loop.
