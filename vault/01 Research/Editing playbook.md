## Music
- A low **bed** under the whole clip, ducked under speech (≈ -24 dB under voices, louder in gaps).
- **Suspense** track during the build/silence, **cut music to silence right before the punchline** (makes the vine boom hit), then a **goofy/comedy** track back in for the laugh.
- Brainrot/aura edits use phonk; comedy podcast clips use quirky tracks. We use royalty-free Kevin MacLeod tracks (see [[SFX and music library]]).

## Effects vocabulary
- **Punch-in zoom** 1.0 → 1.15–1.4 on emphasis; slow push 1.0 → 1.05 inside every shot.
- **Stacked layout**: wide shot top third + close-up below (streamer/podcast clips, Ref A).
- **Red circle** around a person in the wide shot (who the clip is about).
- **Color flash**: green = punchline/win, red = fail. Fade from ~70 % to ~10 % over 0.6 s.
- **Bokeh/particles** during the flash (Ref A) or floating emoji during laughs.
- **B&W + zoom** on a reaction face, often with "AURA" counter.
- **Screen shake** for 4–6 frames on the vine boom.
- **Whip/motion blur** between scenes.
- **Asterisk captions** for sounds/actions: `*SIGHS*`, `*LAUGHS*`, `*THINKING*`.

## Memes that show up most
💀 skull sticker · troll face · "AURA ±N" counter · "ARE YOU SURE?" card · "MISSION PASSED! RESPECT +" · 🗿 moai · "THIS ONE" twist label · cut-out real images of the thing mentioned (goat, Jesus, Hulk).
