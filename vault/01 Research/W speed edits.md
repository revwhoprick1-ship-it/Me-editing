# W Speed edits (wholesome podcast moments)

User: "when it's wholesome use a *W speed* edit but with our same editing style". The song is **"Blue" by Yung Kai** (`public/music/blue-yung-kai.mp3`, from SoundCloud).

Analysed: `vWrSsM8JBhc` ("Ishowspeed Picks Up Water - W Speed ❤️‍🩹 Edit Be Like", 44K views), `cAF_VTXjYuI`, `iphdwlOx--c`, plus a search of ~40 "W Speed" Shorts. Storyboard: `assets/research/wspeed_vWrSsM8JBhc.jpg`.

## The formula
1. **Narrated action text.** A small, normal action is captioned like breaking news: italic, in asterisks, typed on word by word. Examples: `*ISHOWSPEED PICKS UP WATER*`, `*SPEED PUTS WATER BACK*`, `*INHALE*` / `*EXHALE*`. The subject's name is red, the rest white.
2. **Glow ring.** A cyan/teal ring highlights the trivial object or action.
3. **The "W" slam.** On the beat, a huge **W SPEED** in bold, with a colour per beat (red → green → cyan) and the **❤️‍🩹 mending heart** emoji next to it. For us this becomes **W TREVOR ❤️‍🩹**.
4. **Crowd cutaways.** On every beat after the W slam, it cuts back and forth between the subject and a crowd going crazy (cheering crowds, celebrations). Each shot lasts 0.3–0.6 s. We use our own **celebration/scream clips from the hooks folder** (Backyard Breaks guys celebrating) as the crowd.
5. **Meme overlays** like "We make the right person famous 💻": a small sticker in a corner.
6. **Pacing.** Calm build (normal speed, soft part of Blue), then the W slam lands where the song's beat/vocal comes in, and the cuts follow the beat.

## How we combine it with our style
- Keep the podcast layout (black bars, top caption, glow captions with iOS emoji, whoosh/ding).
- Music = Blue instead of Du bist gut genug, and the "W TREVOR ❤️‍🩹" slam on the wholesome peak.
