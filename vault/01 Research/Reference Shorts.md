## Ref A — Backyard Breaks podcast clip (same show as the user's footage)
https://youtube.com/shorts/Tr1D4tT9zhI · frames: `assets/references/Tr1D4tT9zhI_hi.jpg`, `Tr1D4tT9zhI_02–04.jpg`

**This is the main style to copy.**
- **Hook title** at the very top, the whole video: small yellow uppercase with glow, e.g. "WAIT FOR PICK #1…".
- **Font:** geometric bold sans, uppercase (Montserrat ExtraBold 800). **No black outline:** soft **white glow** around white words. Colored keywords glow in their own color.
- **Keyword colors:** yellow (setup words, "TREVOR'S DREAM"), green (the payoff noun, "GOLF FOURSOME?", "TIGER"), plus blue/pink in other frames. 1–3 keywords per sentence, never the whole line.
- **Captions** sit in the middle of the lower half, 2–4 words, words appear as they are said (unsaid words stay hidden or dim).
- **Layout:** full-frame close-up of whoever talks, *or* stacked (wide table shot in the top third, close-up underneath). The vertical frame has a blurred, zoomed copy of the video behind it.
- **Red circle** drawn around the person being talked about in the wide shot.
- **Punchline:** the whole frame tinted **green**, yellow/orange bokeh dots floating, a real cut-out image of the thing mentioned (a goat for "GOAT"), and a yellow label on the split line between the stacked shots: `*JESUS + TIGER*`.
- B&W meme photo cutaway (old man with hands up) as a reaction beat.
- Fast jump cuts between close-up and wide.

## Ref B — piano cat
https://youtube.com/shorts/H_5vKixarGA · frames: `assets/references/H_5vKixarGA_hi.jpg`

- **Split screen:** speaker top, B-roll bottom; the split line is where the caption sits.
- **One word at a time**, lowercase, **teal serif with teal glow** (soft bold serif, think "DM Serif Display").
- **iOS emoji** above the word that names a thing (🎹 piano, 🕰 before, 🎬, 🚫 nobody, ⌛ now).
- B-roll changes on nearly every noun (AI images of the cat/piano), glitch effect on the last one.

## Ref C: podcast layout (user: "for podcast style videos make it like this")
https://youtube.com/shorts/TpwR9FMWQg4 · frames: `assets/references/TpwR9FMWQg4_hi.jpg`. It's the same NBA GOAT clip edited by someone else.
- **Black bars** top and bottom; the video sits in a box (~1080×1150, top at y≈390).
- **Plain caption on the top bar**: sentence case, white, bold, no glow, + iOS 💀: "Do you agree with this? 💀". It stays the whole video.
- Small lowercase one-word captions (teal/yellow/green) near face height.
- **Cut-out photos** of the people mentioned pop up at the bottom of the box: Kobe/LeBron/Jordan on "NBA GOAT", Dellavedova when he's named, overlapping into the bottom bar.

**How we apply it (user's instruction):** keep OUR edit style (glow captions, iOS emoji, flash, SFX, music, aura etc.) and ADD Ref C's black bars + box, plain top caption, and cut-out photos. Don't switch to Ref C's small captions.
