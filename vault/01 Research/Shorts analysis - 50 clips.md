## How this was gathered
- Source: YouTube hashtag Shorts feeds `#memeedit #trollface #kaicenat #ishowspeed #funnymoments #funnyedit #podcastclips #podcast #sportsmemes #streamerclips #nflmemes #clippingchannel` (328 candidates, see `assets/research/pool.txt`).
- Picked the top ~5 by views per hashtag (`final50.txt`). 10 of those only had 48×27 px storyboards (unreadable), so they were swapped for the next most-viewed clips in the clipping-relevant hashtags (`repl_used.txt`).
- **What was actually looked at:** YouTube storyboards (≈1 frame every ~0.3–1 s at 101×180 px) for every clip, contact sheets in `assets/research/g01–g26.jpg` and `r1–r5.jpg`, plus full-res frames of the two reference Shorts.
- **Limitation — no audio:** from this cloud container YouTube refuses to serve audio/video (bot check), only storyboards. So cuts, text, colors, stickers, layouts and effects are *observed*; **sound effects and music are inferred** from what's on screen (e.g. a 💀 sticker slamming in = the classic vine-boom moment) plus standard meme-edit conventions. Marked *(inferred)* wherever it matters.
