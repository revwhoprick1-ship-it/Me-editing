## Hook / top banner (D, E, F)
- **White box** across the top (D, F) or **black bar** (E) holding the hook for the **whole video**.
- Heavy black uppercase text, 2 lines, **1–2 colored keywords**: count/rarity in **red** ("ONLY 10", "LIFETIME"), money in **green** ("$1,500").
- Formula: *scarcity + money + who* ("ONLY 10 OF THIS $1,500 NBA RISING STAR!"), or a *dare* ("BET YOU DON'T KNOW THIS 1/1").

## Captions
- D/E/F: white uppercase, ~2–3 words, **center of the frame**, heavy dark drop shadow (not a glow), one keyword green or teal.
- G: *asterisk narration* (`*REMEMBERS THAT ONE GIRL*`) in italic yellow / pink / green **with strong glow**, 2 lines.

## Red circle & red arrow (tracking), F and D/E
- **What:** thick red ring (~10 px) around a **face**; a big chunky **red arrow with a dark shadow** pointing diagonally at the subject (D/E point at the card corner / number, F points at the kid).
- **When:** the first 1–2 s (F opens on it: "THIS KID…") and on the moment the thing matters (the serial number "10" in D, the corner of the pack in E).
- **How:** the circle **follows the face** as he moves (tracked); during a punch-in the circle grows with the frame. The arrow sits just outside the circle/object, pointing in from the lower right, and jabs in (slides ~40 px toward the target and back) so the eye goes there.
- **Why:** tells a viewer with no context *who/what to look at* in a busy frame.

## Transitions
- **Motion-blur whip** (horizontal streak smear, 3–5 frames) between every jump cut and between scenes (D storyboard: blurred frames before the green-wall cut; F: "HAPPENED" over a streaked frame).
- **Cutaway to a reaction/explainer shot** (host at the green wall in D and E) in the middle of the pull, to restart attention, then whip back.
- **Punch-in zoom** on the card at the reveal.
- G: soft dissolve + light leak / lens flare for emotional story beats.
- **Where:** every cut point. **Why:** hides jump cuts and resets attention every 1–3 s.

## Card reveal (D, E)
- Big **card overlay** (the card filling the middle) with a **stacked title** above it: player (white) / parallel+number (red) / **price (green)**, e.g. "ACE BAILEY / BLACK TO 10 / $1,500+", "CYCLOPS / 1/1 SKETCH".
- Exclamation caption under it ("HOLY MOLY" with green MOLY).
- End: engagement question ("HOW MUCH DO YOU THINK IS THIS CARD?") + 😮 emoji.
