# Hooks: heyDominik "give me 15 minutes, I'll make your hooks 89% better" (user upload 2026-11-23)
- Four hook elements must speak to ONE viewer: visual hook (what's on screen), text hook, spoken hook (first words said), first 6 words of the caption.
- Instant clarity in the first 0.5 s: who it's for + what it's about. Text hook on screen from frame 1, no animation, no delay. Swipes in the first 0.5 s = wrong audience (algorithm retargets); swipes a bit later = bad reel (reach to non-followers drops).
- Curiosity gap by 2-3 s: the viewer must have one question they need answered.
- Strangers don't know you: vague hooks ("this insane thing happened") only hold followers.
- Find novel angles: break expectations / challenge the status quo; avoid copy-paste hook lists (people swipe faster on hooks they've seen).
- Skip rate also depends on WHO saw it, not only the hook.
- Next: secondary hooks / pattern interrupts after the hook (his follow-up video).
