## A · Common editing identity (in all or nearly all four)
1. **Text from frame 0, or within 1.5 s.** The video never starts "clean" [4/4].
2. **ALL-CAPS heavy sans captions, centered, 1–2 lines** [4/4].
3. **`*asterisk*` action captions** to narrate what's happening [4/4].
4. **Color-coded keywords** (red danger, green good, cyan narration, yellow questions, magenta twist) [3/4: V1, V2, V3].
5. **Flashes or color washes on emotional beats** [3/4].
6. **Digital punch-ins to create "angles"** out of a single camera [4/4].
7. **A clear payoff in the final 15% of runtime**, marked by a music change or graphic [4/4].
8. **Something new on screen at least every second** [4/4].

## B · Unique techniques
- **V0:** flash-forward cold open; freeze + darken + muffle; speaker labels in two colors; blurred-background fill.
- **V1:** per-word fade-in captions with glow; flag-colored words; B&W→color; whip pan; white-frame + zoom-blur outro; "aura" bloom card; fade to black.
- **V2:** voice-over storytelling; small 2-word captions; meme stickers; bobbing 3D arrow; "MOMENTS LATER" time-skip flash; animated like CTA.
- **V3:** typewriter hook; spotlight vignette; low-pass tension sections; green "SAVED" wash + ding; "BUT THEN HE DID THIS..." pre-reveal cliffhanger; heart pop.

## Most distinctive (most "branded" looking)
1. V3's **green wash + ding** on the payoff.
2. V0's **freeze + muffle + riser** hook.
3. V1's **glowing color-coded word-by-word captions**.

## C · Worth combining
- **V0 cold open + freeze** with **V3's typewriter title and arrow**: a "what happened" hook that also points at the subject.
- **V3's muffle-during-tension → open + ding on payoff** with **V1's color washes**. One audio-visual "emotion" system: muffled = tension; open + colored wash + ding = release.
- **V2's narration structure** (premise → problem → escalation ×3 → payoff → CTA) with **V1/V3 caption styling** in place of V2's tiny captions.
- **V3's `BUT THEN HE DID THIS...` cliffhanger** before any final reveal, whatever the format.

## D · Don't combine (they clash)
- **Boxed captions (V0) with glow captions (V1/V3).** Two caption systems look like two different channels.
- **Word-by-word fade captions with typewriter captions in the same section.** Pick one per role: typewriter for titles only.
- **Voice-over with dense dialogue captions.** V2 drops the VO whenever the original audio matters. Don't caption both at once.
- **More than one colored flash within ~3 s.** The references space them 5+ s apart (V1). Frequent flashes turn into noise and fatigue the eye.
- **Meme stickers with the serious "aura" grade/outro.** The tones conflict.
- **Mixing the −6 LUFS crushed master with the quieter edits.** Keep one loudness target (−14 LUFS).
