What if every human could fly? First, you'd need wings six to seven meters wide. Longer than a car. Plus a chest packed with flight muscle, like a bird. Even then, scientists think you couldn't flap your way up. You'd mostly glide. Above two and a half kilometers, thin air can make you sick. And every kilometer up is about six degrees colder. The sky is already crowded. US planes hit over twenty thousand animals a year. Now add eight billion flying people. Would you still want wings?
